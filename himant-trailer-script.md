### RUDHRA (Hero)
- 24 years old, lean-muscular tribal build
- Long dreadlocks with ice crystals and bone beads

- Fur and bone scale armor — lightweight, built for speed
- Stone axe — Trishul symbol etched on the wooden haft
- **SIGNATURE DETAIL**: Piercing blue eyes — unusual, unforgettable

### RAKTASUR (Villain)
- 38 years old, massive frame, warlord build
- Heavy skull-adorned bone armor (skulls of enemies)
- Dark red wolf-fur cape
- Face covered in dark geometric tribal tattoos
- **SIGNATURE DETAIL**: Diamond rune carved into forehead (not tattooed — physically carved, scarred)
- **SIGNATURE DETAIL**: Blue eyes — same exact shade as Rudhra's

### VIRANGI (Female Lead)
- 22 years old, warrior's build — fast and precise
- Shaved sides, long warrior braid running down center-back
- Obsidian glass twin blades (volcanic glass — razor sharp)

- 65+ years old, thin but not frail — deep stillness
- Long silver-white hair, loose
- White ash marking on forehead (full Trishul symbol)
- Heavy fur robes — the most elaborate costume

# AI TOOL WORKFLOW — HOW TO GENERATE THE TRAILER

## Step 1: Generate Stills (Midjourney / Flux)
Use each AI image prompt above. Settings:
`--ar 16:9 --q 2 --style raw --v 6.1`

For consistency: always include the character reference description in every prompt.

## Step 2: Animate (Kling AI / Runway Gen-3)
- Upload your Midjourney still as the first frame
- Prompt the motion: e.g. *"slow push forward, no camera shake, cinematic"*
- Duration: match the shot duration from the table above
- Kling AI recommended for battle shots (handles crowd motion well)
- Runway Gen-3 recommended for character face shots (emotion holds better)

## Step 3: Music (Suno AI)
- Generate the two music tracks using prompts above
- Download as separate files: WAR_SCORE.mp3 and EMOTIONAL_THEME.mp3
- These will be edited together in the final trailer cut

## Step 4: Edit (CapCut / DaVinci Resolve)
- Import all video clips in order from the shot list table
- Lay music tracks as per the Music Map
- Add text cards in white serif font
- Add letterbox bars (top/bottom black bars — 2.39:1 ratio)
- Color grade: cold blue-teal overall, with the ONE warm shot (mother scene) staying amber

## Step 5: Subtitles
- All Hindi dialogue = white text, centered, bottom of frame
- English subtitles = slightly smaller, italic, below Hindi
